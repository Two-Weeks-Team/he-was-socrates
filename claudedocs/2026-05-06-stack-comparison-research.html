🚫 Apple Foundation Models — Why We Cannot Switch

The user's question "is Gemma 4 + MLX best?" demands a fair comparison to Apple's own on-device LLM framework (macOS 26+). We checked. It's a dead end for our use case.

DimensionApple Foundation ModelsGemma 4 E4B + MLX (ours)
Parameters~3B, 2-bit QAT mixed-bit LoRA ~3.7 bpw~4.5B effective, 4-bit MLX
Korean supportBeta (PFIGSCJK group)140+ pre-train languages
System promptGuardrails non-disablable, applied to BOTH input AND outputVerbatim Korean Socratic prompt embedded at compile-time
Context window4,096 tokens TOTAL (instructions + transcript + tools + response, all cumulative)256K (E4B has 128K; family up to 256K)
Function callingTool protocol, @Generable constrained decodingNative function-calling, our 4-function dispatch

Min OSmacOS 26 / iOS 26macOS 14+

Three hard blockers (priority order)

  1. Guardrails are non-disablable and dual-sided. Per WWDC25 #248: "a default guardrail is always applied… to both input and output." Our entire defer_to_human abstention mechanic — load-bearing for legal/medical/financial/welfare/insurance/emergency — depends on a precisely tuned Korean Socratic prompt. Apple's guardrails will rewrite, soften, or refuse outputs unpredictably. Violates invariant #2 (abstention is the product) and #3 (Korean tone locked).
  2. 4K cumulative context. Cannot hold verbatim system prompt + 4 tool schemas + non-trivial wondering-log payload simultaneously. function_call_contract.yaml already approaches this budget on a single turn. Mitigation M01 (256K context for multi-year recall) is impossible.
  3. macOS 26 minimum. Strands every macOS 14/15 user.

🧠 UX Literature — Why 6s is a Feature, Not a Bug

The decisive paper

Tan, F. F-Y., & Nov, O. (CHI 2026, April 24, 2026, n=240, between-subjects).
Latencies 2 s vs 9 s vs 20 s with identical response content. Findings:
  • 2 s rated less thoughtful, less useful
  • 9 s and 20 s rated more favorably — users attributed pause to "thinking"
  • Behavior unchanged — only subjective perception moved
  • Authors frame this as "positive friction"
Our 6 s sits BETWEEN the 2 s "feels shallow" and 9 s "feels thoughtful" conditions, sliding toward favorable.

Supporting literature

  • Stivers et al. PNAS 2009 — humans deliver dispreferred / contradictory / "I don't know" responses 100–500 ms slower than confirmations. Latency itself signals deliberation. Our Korean Socratic abstention persona naturally reproduces this.
  • Khan Academy Khanmigo (2024–2025) — production AI tutor running in 3–6 s response bracket. Same league as us, treated as shippable. Engineering blog reports 0.3-3 s deltas across 1.35M threads as wins, not blockers.
  • arXiv 2601.16720 Watching AI Think (2026) — visible thinking indicators raise perceived competence and trust. Our PR-γ "thinking pulse" animation on the bust during Phase.thinking is mandatory, not decorative.
  • Nielsen 10s rule — 6 s sits well inside the attention-retention window. Variance matters more than mean: keep p95 below 9 s.
Don't market on speed; market on what nothing-leaves-the-device buys you. The 6 s is the price of the privacy property. Cloud frontier 200–500 ms is reachable only by streaming Korean private dialogue to GPU clusters — exactly what our spec forbids.

Sources: Tan & Nov CHI 2026 · Stivers PNAS 2009 · Khan Academy AI Tutor · arXiv 2601.16720 · NN/g Response Time Limits

Decisive answers

  • (a) Working Swift impl today? No. Only mlx-community/speculative-decoding is Swift+SPM, but it's a generic two-model scheme — never tested with Gemma 4. We'd be the first to wire MTP drafter through mlx-swift-lm.

Hard floor analysis

  • Pure inference floor (research-published): ~1.5–2 s for 4B 4-bit MLX on M1 Max (per arXiv 2511.05502 scaled estimate). Decoding ≈ 1 s; TTFT ≈ 0.3 s on cached prompt; tax ≈ 0.5 s.
  • Application floor: STT endpointing ~300–500 ms + AVSpeechSynthesizer prep ~50–100 ms + viseme schedule negligible + function-call parse <5 ms ≈ 0.5 s on top of inference.
  • Hardware floor: M1 Max GPU bandwidth 400 GB/s caps both decode and prefill at roughly half M3 Ultra. Moving to M3 Ultra / M4 Max / M5 yields ~2–3× speedup with no code change.
  • Below ~2.5 s/turn on M1 Max for Gemma 4 E4B 4-bit is not achievable without changing model size, quantization tier (frozen invariant), or hardware.
The 6 s isn't the LLM. Per agent #10 synthesis, ~4 s of our 6 s is STT + function-call orchestration + TTS prep + viseme — not Gemma decode. If Tier 1 doesn't move the needle as projected, profile the turn loop end-to-end before more LLM-side work.
