Why this matters

The midwife, not the oracle.

Most assistants race to answer. This one practices maieutics — Socrates' method of returning a question that helps you find the answer yourself.

So when you ask something only a human should answer — a doctor, a lawyer, a person who knows your life — the bust says so. That refusal is the product, not a bug.
