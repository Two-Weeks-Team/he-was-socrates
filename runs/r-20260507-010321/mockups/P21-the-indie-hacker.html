03Frequently asked

How do you make money?
We don't. Donations to the Two-Weeks-Team collective fund the developers' coffee and the HuggingFace bandwidth on first-launch weight pulls. Nothing else.
Why are all three tiers $0?
Every CLAUDE.md invariant — NO-CLOUD, abstention-as-product, Korean 평어체 lock, no photoreal — refuses paid-tier gating. The privacy invariant and the zero-cost invariant turn out to be the same invariant.
Can I cancel?
There is no subscription to cancel. Drag the .app to Trash; remove ~/Library/Application Support/HeWasSocrates. The wondering log goes with it. No data is held remotely because no data was ever sent remotely.
Do you offer enterprise SSO?
No. macOS user accounts are the auth boundary. The model runs under each user's sandbox. There is nothing to federate.
